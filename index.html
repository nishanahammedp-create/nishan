ℹ️ About ALERT 360

Overview

ALERT 360 is a comprehensive disaster alert and emergency management application designed to keep you safe and informed during critical situations.

Key Features

  • 🚨 Real-time Active Alerts for various disaster types
  • 📢 Nearby Events and Emergency Notifications
  • 📞 Quick Access to Emergency Contacts
  • 💡 Safety Tips and Disaster Prevention Guidelines
  • 🆘 Emergency SOS with one-tap alert system
  • 📍 Location-based alert customization
  • 🔔 Customizable notification preferences

Supported Alert Types

Weather Alerts • Earthquake Alerts • Flood Warnings • Traffic/Accident Alerts

Emergency Services

Police: 100/911 | Ambulance: 102/911 | Fire Brigade: 101/911 | Disaster Management: 108/911
