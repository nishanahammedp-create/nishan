## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Open the App**: Open `index.html` in any modern web browser
2. **View Alerts**: See real-time disaster alerts in your area
3. **Emergency Contact**: Tap any emergency contact in the contacts grid or SOS tab
4. **Customize Settings**: Click settings (⚙️) to adjust preferences
5. **Update Location**: Enter your city in settings to get location-specific alerts
6. **Check Status**: View your safety status at the top - 🟢 Safe, 🟡 Warning, 🔴 Danger
